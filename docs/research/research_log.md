## 3. Proposed Data Source
**FAOSTAT – Food and Agriculture Organization Corporate Statistical Database**

Website: https://www.fao.org/faostat/

The project intends to use historical crop production statistics, including production quantities, harvested areas, and yields.

## 4. Initial Research Findings
Agricultural production data can help identify crop yield trends, compare crop performance, and understand changes in production over time.

AgriYield will use an Oracle database to organize and analyze agricultural records and Power BI to present the results through interactive dashboards.

## 5. Initial Database Design
- CROPS
- COUNTRIES
- AGRICULTURAL_SEASONS
- HARVESTED_AREAS
- CROP_PRODUCTION
- YIELD_ANALYSIS

## 6. Research Limitations
The initial project will focus on available historical and country-level agricultural statistics. These records should not be interpreted as individual farm-level production data.
